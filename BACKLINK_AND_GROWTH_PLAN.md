# Backlink & Growth Plan for getnetworthcalculator.com

Goal: take the site from 8 clicks/week (day 20) to a sustainable 1,000+ clicks/month, then monetize with ads.

The single biggest lever at this stage is **authority (backlinks)**. On-page SEO is done; Google has shown it will index and rank your 589 pages (delaware pos 5.5, portland pos 1, iowa pos 3.9). What's missing is trust signals to push the 40-50 position country pages (UK/Canada/Australia) onto page 1.

## Phase 1 — Free links (this month)

### 1. Directory & listicle submissions (2-3 hours)
| Target | Why | What to submit |
|---|---|---|
| [Carrd-like tool directories] | Instant indexed links | The calculator URL |
| Hacker News (Show HN) | Potential viral spike | "Show HN: I built a privacy-first net worth percentile calculator" |
| Product Hunt | One-time traffic + backlink | Launch page |
| AlternativeTo | Tool directory | Calculator listing |
| Google Sheets "free calculator" roundups | Niche relevance | Link + description |
| Foundr / Sidebar lists | Small authority | Link |

### 2. Content assets that earn links organically
- **Publish 3 "linkable" studies** using your Fed SCF data:
  - "What $500k really means in every US state" (data visualization, embeddable table)
  - "Net worth required to be top 1% in each of the 50 states" (interactive map)
  - "Age-by-age wealth milestones: are you on track?"
- These get picked up by personal-finance blogs and Reddit. Each embeddable table has `data-lang="..."` iframe embed code already on your homepage — the embed IS the link magnet.
- Add an **OG image generator** so every shared result card has a branded thumbnail (currently og-image.png is static).

### 3. Reddit / Quora / social seeding (careful, no spam)
- Reddit: r/personalfinance, r/Fire, r/PersonalFinanceCanada, r/UKPersonalFinance — answer questions with data, link only where genuinely helpful.
- Quora: "What is the average net worth by age?" style answers.
- Pinterest: pin the state benchmark tables (finance/wealth boards get traffic).
- X/Twitter: your site already has "𝕏 Share" — post one benchmark graphic per week with the link.

## Phase 2 — Authority (months 2-3)

### 4. Digital PR
- Pitch to personal finance journalists (Bankrate, The Motley Fool, Yahoo Finance contributors) using your unique state-by-state data.
- Offer "you can embed our calculator" — reciprocal links.

### 5. Guest posts
- 2-3 guest posts/month on personal-finance blogs with author bio linking back.

### 6. HARO / Connectively responses
- Respond to journalist queries about net worth statistics. One good quote = a real editorial link.

## Phase 3 — Internal growth loops (do these now, they're free)

### 7. Cross-link the ranking pages harder
- The pages at pos 1-10 (portland, iowa, delaware, north-carolina) should link to your closest-to-page-1 pages (usa-vs-canada pos 10, net-worth-percentile pos 8). Link equity flows where it converts.
- Every calculator result should offer a "Compare to [your state]" CTA linking to the state page.

### 8. Publish frequency
- Add 2-4 new data-backed pages/month (new calculators, new countries, new states). Fresh content keeps Google crawling.

## Monetization timing (do NOT do yet)
- Wait until ≥ 1,000 clicks/month (~100+ clicks/day) before applying to AdSense.
- Realistic trajectory: US/UK finance RPM $15-40. At 1,000 clicks/month you'd earn roughly $50-150/month from ads. At 10,000 clicks/month: $500-1,500/month.
- Better monetization than display ads for this niche: **affiliate** (brokerages, robo-advisors like Wealthfront/SoFi, credit cards) — finance affiliates pay $30-100+ per lead, far more than RPM ads.
